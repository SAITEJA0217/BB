# Student User Guide

Welcome to **StudyMate Offline**! This guide will help you navigate your new desktop study assistant.

## 🔍 Searching for Information
1. Open the application. You will be greeted by the **Search** interface.
2. Type your question or topic into the search bar (e.g., "What is database normalization?").
3. StudyMate uses advanced offline algorithms (like spell-correction and synonym expansion) to find the exact paragraph in your syllabus/PDFs.
4. Click on a result to instantly jump to the exact page in the embedded **PDF Viewer**.

## 🔖 Bookmarking Important Concepts
- When reading a useful search result, click the **Bookmark Icon** located at the top-right of the result card.
- Navigate to the **Bookmarks Page** (using the left sidebar) to view all your saved concepts.
- Click **Export CSV** to download your bookmarks into a spreadsheet for later review.

## ⚙️ Customizing Your Experience
Navigate to the **Settings** tab in the sidebar to configure:
- **Theme**: Light, Dark, or System.
- **Zoom Level**: Adjust the default zoom of the embedded PDF viewer.
- **Highlight Color**: Choose how search results are highlighted on the page.
